# Pan and Tilt full-turn operation

Firmware `V4.0_HOLD_FREE360` advertises `FREE360_CAP:1` and accepts absolute
Pan and Tilt targets from -360 to +360 degrees relative to the current ZERO.
Both axes can therefore scan 0 to 360, or reverse from 360 to 0. Targets
outside this range and nonfinite targets are rejected; this is not unlimited
multi-turn rotation.

MATLAB uses the reported full-turn capabilities without applying stored travel
ranges, the historical Tilt -5..95 fallback, or the 3D travel confirmation gate.
Releasing or resetting still requires Lock + ZERO. STOP, holding watchdog,
encoder validity and movement checks remain active. Firmware without
`FREE360_CAP:1` retains its previous behavior.

The spherical 3D coordinate model still requires theta in 0..180 degrees;
this coordinate rule does not restrict a standalone EL motor scan to 180.

Updated native hold tests cover Tilt +/-360 and reject 361. MATLAB
`TestFree360` covers capability decoding, travel selection after ZERO and
full-turn validation through the serial fixture.
